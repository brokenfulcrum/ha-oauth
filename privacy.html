Privacy Policy

This app is used only by its owner to connect their own Google Calendar to their personal Home Assistant server.

  • Calendar data is accessed only to display and automate the owner's own events in Home Assistant.
  • Data stays on the owner's self-hosted server and is not sold, shared, or transferred to any third party.
  • No other users' data is collected.
  • Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page.
